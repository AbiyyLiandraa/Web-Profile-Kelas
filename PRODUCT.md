# Product

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform

web

## Stack

static HTML/CSS/JavaScript

## Users

Students and classmates visiting to recognize the identity of class X.2 and browse shared class memories.

## Product Purpose

A class profile page that introduces X.2 and preserves its expedition-themed record and photographs in one approachable place.

## Positioning

The page combines a playful classification record with an interactive trail-camera archive, making the class feel like a documented expedition rather than a generic roster.

## Operating Context

Visitors arrive in a browser, scan the class record, scroll through expedition photos, trigger available trail-camera frames, and open the complete photo archive in Google Drive.

## Capabilities and Constraints

The existing page is a static web surface. Preserve the Indonesian copy, class facts, available photo assets, population counter, verification stamp, trail-camera interaction, scroll navigation, and Google Drive archive link. Some future photos may be unavailable and must remain clearly marked as coming soon.

## Brand Commitments

The class identity is X.2 at SMAN 1 Kota Depok for the 2026/2027 school season, with Bu Ratna as wali kelas. The expedition and jungleland framing is an established voice in the current page.

## Evidence on Hand

Existing content and code in index.html, including fotocam1.jpg and fotocam2.jpg references. No additional photo claims or testimonials are available.

## Product Principles

- Make the class identity recognizable within seconds.
- Treat memories as a living archive, not decoration.
- Keep available and unavailable photos unmistakably distinct.
- Preserve playful interactions without compromising keyboard and mobile access.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

The page must remain usable on mobile, support keyboard activation for interactive frames, respect reduced-motion preferences, and retain visible focus states.
